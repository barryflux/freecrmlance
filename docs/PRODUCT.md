# PRODUCT

## Vision
Freecrmlance is a modular SaaS for freelancers to manage the complete client relationship: clients, contacts, history, documents and secure sharing, quotes, invoices and payments, with audits later.

**Un client, un espace, toute la relation.**

It is not a full ERP. It centralizes the operational client relationship while remaining simple.

## Initial user and problem
A freelancer wants simple client/admin relationship management without fragmented spreadsheets, cloud storage, email, invoicing tools and notes.

## Value
Centralization, simplicity, traceability, modularity and useful automation.

## Platform
Cross-cutting identity/authentication, Workspace, permissions, activity/logging and configuration/notifications as needed.

## CRM
Clients, companies/professional individuals, contacts, administrative data, notes, history and missions/projects when introduced.

## Documents
Upload, download, classification, customer association, explicit sharing, access and generated documents. Distinguish USER_DOCUMENT from SYSTEM_DOCUMENT. Customer association never implies sharing.

## Client Portal
A secure, simpler client-facing view of explicitly shared documents, quotes, invoices, statuses and actions. It is a controlled projection, not a second business system.

## Billing
Service catalog, quotes/lines, quote-to-invoice, invoices/lines, credit notes, PDFs, statuses, due dates and payments. Electronic invoicing/compliance is a separate regulatory layer.

## Audit
Not MVP. Future generic engine: templates, sections, controls, evidence, findings, recommendations, reports. It reuses CRM/Documents/Platform.

## MVP
Account -> Workspace -> Customer -> Contacts -> Documents -> Sharing -> Quote -> Invoice -> Payment Tracking.

## Initially out of scope
Full ERP, accounting, payroll/HR, complex CRM, marketing automation, advanced teams, marketplace, native mobile, full audit engine, advanced analytics and many integrations.

## Initial success
A freelancer can create a client, centralize/share documents, create a quote, invoice and track payment without another tool for the base workflow.
